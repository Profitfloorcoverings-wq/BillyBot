Where BillyBot takes a different approach

Instead of a tool you drive, BillyBot does the first draft of the office work and hands it to you to approve.

It starts in your inbox

Connect Gmail or Outlook. Billy reads new enquiries, turns them into jobs with the customer and address attached, files the attachments and drafts a reply in your voice. Nothing goes to a customer until you approve it, unless you switch on auto-send.

Quotes from whatever arrives

A voice note from site, a few typed lines, a forwarded email, a floor plan or a full tender pack, priced on your own supplier prices, markups and labour rates. More on flooring quoting software.

Plans drawn, scanned or read

Draw a floor plan in the web app, including by touch on an iPad, scan rooms with a supported LiDAR iPhone or iPad, or send Billy a photo or PDF of a plan to read into rooms.

Cutting plans off the saved plan

Built from the floor plan you saved, on the roll width of the product you specified and the pile direction you set. Drag pieces to arrange the cuts. More on cutting plan software.

Xero, Sage or QuickBooks

Quotes and invoices are prepared, then go across when you confirm them, never automatically. No accounting software? Billy produces branded PDFs instead.

Paperwork and fitters

Billy writes the RAMS, with a COSHH section from a safety data sheet library. Fitters are free however many you have, on the App Store and Google Play, and what they record on site saves to the phone first.

The rest of the office

Diary entries syncing with Google or Outlook calendars, rotas and time off, monthly pay summaries, purchase orders, stock, supplier invoices, CIS, and a nudge when a quote has gone quiet.

The bit that is hard to picture

An enquiry lands, and the job, the reply and the quote are drafted by the time you look. This is the real app, running on the page.
