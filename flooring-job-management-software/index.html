From first enquiry to final invoice

One job, six stages. What Billy does at each one, and where you step in.

1

Enquiry becomes a job

An email lands in Gmail or Outlook, website forms included. Billy creates the job, links or creates the customer, files the drawings and photos, and drafts a reply for you to approve.

2

Survey and measure

Book the survey, then draw the rooms or scan them with Scan & talk on a supported iPhone. Tender drawings by email? Billy measures from the drawings.

3

Quote on your rates

Billy prices the quote from your own price lists and markups. You check it, then confirm it as a branded PDF or into Xero, Sage or QuickBooks.

4

Send, chase, win

You approve the email. Quotes show Sent, Viewed or Bounced, and if one goes quiet Billy offers a follow-up draft. Customers accept online or by reply, and the job moves to Won.

5

Order and fit

The job card prompts you to order materials, then book the fitting. The diary entry fills in from the job and assigned fitters get a notification.

6

Invoice and get paid

Finished jobs prompt Send invoice. Billy prepares the invoice from the quote; you confirm it into your accounts or as a PDF. Overdue invoices get flagged to chase.
