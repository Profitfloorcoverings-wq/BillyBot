There's no single best flooring software, only the best fit for the way your business wins and runs work. This guide covers what to look for, then profiles eleven options side by side, using each supplier's own website.

Start with the kind of flooring business you run

The right software depends on where your week goes. Decide which of these sounds most like you; plenty of businesses are a mix.

Domestic fitter or small fitting firm

You measure, quote and fit, often from the van. Prioritise fast quotes on your own rates, cutting maths you trust, a diary, and invoices that reach your accounts without re-typing.

Flooring shop or showroom

You sell from a showroom and send estimators out. Prioritise estimating in the store and the customer's home, stock and remnants, deposits and a fitting diary. Shop-focused systems are built around this.

Commercial contractor

You price from drawings, specifications and bills of quantities. Prioritise PDF take-off, cost-plus or rates-based pricing, CIS and domestic reverse charge VAT, and keeping up with tender emails.

What to look for, and what to ask

Every brochure promises to save you time. These questions show whether a system will work for your jobs.

Quotes on your own prices

Can it hold your supplier prices, markups and labour rates, and price a real job without re-keying? See how to quote a flooring job.

Estimating from drawings

Can it take off areas from a PDF drawing, and let you check every room before a price goes out? More on flooring estimating software.

Cutting plans and roll widths

Does it compare roll widths, handle stairs and pile direction, and give fitters a plan to cut from? See cutting plan software.

Diary and fitting

Can you book surveys and fittings against the job, and do fitters hear when their day changes?

Email and enquiries

Most work arrives by email. Does the software work from your inbox, or does someone copy every enquiry in by hand?

Accounting integration

Does it send invoices into Xero, Sage or QuickBooks, and can you check each one before it's posted?

Mobile for fitters

Is there a proper fitter app, does each fitter cost extra, and does it cope without signal on site?

What the customer sees

A clear quote, an easy way to accept it, and online deposits if you take them.

Price and terms

Per user, per site or per seat? Check minimum terms, add-ons and data export, not just the headline price.

Flooring measuring, planning and estimating

Flooring estimating

MasterPiece

Measuring, planning and estimating software for floor coverings, sold as modules, for retail (in-store and in-home), contract and new-build work, and insurance validation. Its website says it gained its first customer in 1998.

Strengths
Draw rooms freehand, plan pieces by drag and drop or autoplanner, and review pile direction and seams with the customer. The contracts version takes off areas by tracing PDF or CAD plans; the retail version emails estimates and creates invoices and fitting sheets on acceptance. Windows, iPad, Mac and Android.
Published pricing
Base system £41 a month for up to three users, one logged in at a time, with support and new versions included; more modules available. Free 30-day trial.
Worth checking
Which modules you need, and whether the Windows (local) or cloud version suits you.
masterpiece-system.com
Flooring estimating and EPOS

PlanIt Flooring

UK-built estimating software (PlanIt Estimating) and a till, stock and sales system (PlanIt EPOS), used alone or together, for flooring retailers, fitters, shops and contract groups.

Strengths
AI room mapping from plans, sketches, photos or spoken sizes; roll optimisation across 2m to 5m widths with cutting plans and fitter cutting sheets; quotes with online acceptance and a deposit link. The Business plan adds a till, roll and remnant stock and a Xero-ready invoice export. The app installs from the browser on iPhone and Android and works offline.
Published pricing
From 1 October 2026, excluding VAT: Free (one user, 10 quotes a month); Estimate £19 or Professional £35 per user a month; Business £99 per site a month; Commercial from £299 a month. Three-month minimum.
Worth checking
Which plan includes the features you need.
planitflooring.com
Flooring quoting

FloorQuoter

Quoting software built for UK flooring shops and fitters, with plans for solo operators, small teams and established firms.

Strengths
Step-by-step quotes for carpet rolls, sheet vinyl, LVT and laminate packs, stairs, fitting and extras, with VAT applied. It compares roll widths, seams and waste, keeps internal cutting plans off the customer's quote, and turns accepted quotes into invoice drafts.
Published pricing
Starter £19 a month (25 quotes), Growth £39 (75 quotes, up to 3 users, cutting plans and worksheets) or Pro £79 (500 quotes, up to 10 users), all plus VAT. 14-day free trial.
Worth checking
Each plan's monthly quote allowance against the number of quotes you send.
floorquoter.com
Cut planning and quoting

CarpetCutPro

A browser-based planning, materials and quoting tool for flooring fitters and small flooring shops, built inside a flooring shop in Kent.

Strengths
Carpet planning with 4m and 5m roll comparison, straight stairs and winders; sheet vinyl, LVT and laminate planners; materials lists; PDF quotes and invoices; splitting jobs across dye lots; and supplier price import from Excel or CSV. Mobile and desktop, nothing to install.
Published pricing
£25 a month with unlimited jobs and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
Worth checking
It's a focused planning and quoting tool, so decide what else you need around it.
carpetcutpro.co.uk

Flooring shop and business management

Flooring retail management

Computers for Flooring (Carman)

Cloud-based business management software for carpet shops and flooring retailers, from single stores to multi-site stockholders. Based in Cardiff and part of the ClearCourse group.

CRM with SMS, quotes and invoices with payment links, a fitter diary and the Carman Companion app for iOS and Android. Exports to Sage, Xero and QuickBooks, with 24/7 support in the monthly licence.

Not published on its website; demos on request.

Hosted or installed on site, with no charge per concurrent user.

General job management

Tradify

Published pricing

General job management

Fergus

Job management software with a UK flooring page, for installers from sole operators to crews of 50 or more. Its FAQ notes that a retail business with a showroom may be better suited by purpose-built retail tools.

Published pricing
Essentials from £39 and Professional from £45 per user a month; no lock-in; 14-day free trial.
Worth checking
How your m² rates and roll materials would be set up in its price books.

Published pricing
Excluding VAT: Free for one user (no AI features); Pro £50 a month; Team plans from £200 a month for up to 4 users.

How to run a fair trial

A demo shows software at its best. Your own jobs show whether it works for you.

1

Pick three real jobs

Choose recent jobs you've already priced: one simple, one awkward (stairs, several rooms, mixed floors) and one you lost.

2

Price them on your real costs

Load your actual supplier prices and rates, then compare quantities, cuts, waste and totals against what you sent.

3

Look from both ends

Check the quote your customer receives, and what your fitter sees on their phone on the day of the fit.

4

Check the paperwork and the exit

See how an invoice reaches your accounts, and read the minimum term, add-ons and how you'd export your data if you left.
