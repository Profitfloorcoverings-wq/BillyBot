What to look for in flooring software

Every brochure promises to save you time. These questions show whether a system will actually take work off you.

Quotes on your own prices

Can it hold your supplier prices, markups and labour rates, and price a real job without re-keying? See how to quote a flooring job.

Estimating from drawings

Can it take off areas from a PDF drawing or tender pack, and show you what to check before a price goes out?

Cutting plans and roll widths

Does it work out roll widths, seams and waste, so you order the right amount and fitters have a plan to cut from?

Email and enquiries

Does it work from your inbox and draft the replies, or does someone still copy every enquiry in and write every email by hand?

Accounting

Does it create quotes and invoices in Xero, Sage and QuickBooks, or only export a file? Can you check each one before it's posted?

Fitters

Is there a proper fitter app with bookings, job sheets and RAMS, and does each fitter cost extra?

Diary

Can you book surveys and fittings against the job, and do fitters see their day on their phones?

Follow-ups

Does it show which quotes have been viewed and remind you to chase the ones that have gone quiet?

Price and terms

Per user, per site or per seat? Check what's included, minimum terms and add-ons, not just the headline price.

The kinds of flooring software, and where each one stops

Flooring businesses usually end up choosing between four kinds of tool. Only one of them does the office work for you.

Measuring and quoting apps

Draw the rooms, work out cuts and produce a quote. Useful maths, but the enquiry still has to be typed in, and most of the office work around it is still yours.

Shop and stock systems

Built around the showroom counter: till, stock and remnants. Reading and replying to every enquiry is still on you.

General job apps for every business

Scheduling and invoicing built for any kind of contractor. They aren't built around roll widths, packs, cuts and waste, so flooring maths often ends up in a spreadsheet beside them.
