The kinds of flooring software, and where each one usually stops

Most flooring software is built to store a job, or to speed up one piece of it. When you compare, ask each option what happens between an enquiry landing and the invoice being paid.

Measure and estimate

Measure-and-estimate software

Built to measure rooms, plan cuts and cost a job. It usually ends at the estimate: reading the enquiry, chasing the quote, booking the fitting and raising the invoice are still yours to do. Ask whether it reads your inbox or puts invoices into your accounts.

Shop and stock

Shop and stock systems

Built around the showroom: stock, sales and the till. They usually expect someone to key each job in, so a busy contractor's enquiries, quotes and emails still happen outside them. Ask whether it can price a job from an email, a voice note or a drawing.

General job apps

General job apps built for every kind of business

Scheduling, quotes and invoices for any kind of business. Because they aren't built for flooring, they usually don't work in roll widths, cuts and waste, so a flooring quote still means typing every line. Ask whether it can produce a cutting plan from a floor plan.

Quote apps

Newer quoting apps

Built to get a quote out faster, sometimes with AI. The quote is usually where they stop: the inbox, follow-ups, diary, invoices and site paperwork stay with you. Ask what the software does after the quote is sent.
