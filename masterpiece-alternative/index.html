What MasterPiece does, from its own website

MasterPiece Systems Ltd is based in Epsom, Surrey, and its website says it gained its first customer in 1998. MasterPiece is sold as a suite of modules, so a business buys the capabilities it needs and adds more later. Floor measuring and carpet cutting planning are included in every version. The site describes four main products:

  • In-home measure, plan and cost. Draw rooms freehand and enter the dimensions, plan pieces by drag and drop or with the autoplanner, review pile direction and seam positions with the customer, and calculate “just sufficient” underlay and gripper.
  • In-store retail sales. Email saved estimates to customers; on acceptance, the invoice and fitting sheets are created automatically. It supports tile product sets from ranges including Karndean and Amtico.
  • Contracts and new build. Take off areas by tracing PDF or CAD floor plans on screen, plan whole and part rolls with the “Whole Rolls” autoplanner, add coving allowances, and cost jobs at cost price plus mark-up or on rates.
  • Insurance validation. Measure, plan and cost while with the policy holder, produce a like-for-like replacement estimate, and pass the information back to the insurance centre.

It runs on Windows, with jobs stored on your own machines or server, or as a cloud version on Windows, iPad, Mac and Android. Its purchase pages list a base system at £41 a month for up to three users, one logged in at a time, with support and new versions included. It offers a free 30-day trial, a help desk seven days a week, and training remotely or at your site.

For measuring, planning and estimating, that's a mature, focused product. If it covers the way you work, you may have no reason to change.

Where MasterPiece may suit you better

We'd rather you choose the right tool than switch for the sake of it. From what each product offers, MasterPiece may suit you better if:

  • You mainly need measuring, planning and estimating, and want to buy only those modules. Its published base system price of £41 a month is lower than BillyBot's £149 per office seat.
  • You want software installed on Windows with jobs kept on your own machines or server. BillyBot runs in the cloud, through the web app and the iPhone and Android apps.
  • You carry out insurance claims validation. MasterPiece has a dedicated module for it; BillyBot doesn't.

BillyBot is more likely to suit you if the hours you lose are in the office: enquiries sitting in the inbox, quotes waiting to be written, invoices to key into your accounts and paperwork for site. That's the work Billy takes on, so you can win more work and get your evenings back.

Switching from MasterPiece: what to plan for

Change software without losing track of jobs already on the go.

1

Keep your history

BillyBot doesn't import MasterPiece files. Before changing anything, ask MasterPiece how to export or archive the jobs, plans and customer records you want to keep, and keep access until your open jobs are finished.

2

Bring customers and prices across

Connect Xero, Sage or QuickBooks to sync your customer list. Upload your supplier price lists (PDF, Excel or CSV) and review them before they're applied.

3

Run both for a while

Price new enquiries in BillyBot and let jobs already planned in MasterPiece finish there. Quotes or invoices you still raise directly in Xero, Sage or QuickBooks show up read-only on the customer's open job in BillyBot.

4

Compare real jobs

Re-price three recent jobs in BillyBot and compare areas, roll widths, cuts, underlay, gripper and totals. Check the cutting plan with whoever does your cutting, and the job on a fitter's phone.
