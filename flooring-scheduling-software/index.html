How BillyBot schedules a flooring job

From the first enquiry to the check-in after the fit. Billy prepares, you decide.

1

Enquiry in, survey offered

When an enquiry email needs a survey, Billy's draft reply offers two or three slots checked against the diary, sent when you approve it. Or tell him “survey for Mrs Patel, Tuesday at 1” and it's booked.

2

Quote accepted, dates offered

When a customer accepts your quote, Billy drafts the email offering three free days from the diary. Swap a date, edit the wording, then send.

3

Booked, assigned, notified

The fitting goes in the diary with the fitters on it. They're notified, the job moves to Fitting booked, and it shows in your Google or Outlook calendar if sync is on.

4

On the day, and after

Fitters open the booking on their phone for the address, Maps, the customer's number and notes. After the visit, Billy asks how it went, and “Visit finished” lets him help with the invoice.
